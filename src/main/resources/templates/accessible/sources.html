Your sources

A source is one website or newsletter. Topics are just groups of them, so you can put a source wherever it makes sense to you.

Follow a website

Type the address of a site you like — bbc.com, statnews.com, anything of that shape. You do not need to find an RSS link, and it does not matter if you leave off the https:// part.

For example: nfb.org, statnews.com, applevis.com
