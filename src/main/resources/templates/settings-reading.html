Reading

Choose whether Extrablatt keeps track of what you have read.

When this is on, new articles arrive unread and stay that way until you open one or press Mark all as read under the list. Scrolling and loading more articles never mark anything read.

When it is off, new articles arrive already marked read, so the Unread filter stays empty and the list simply shows everything, newest first.

Page layout

On an e-ink Kindle, the article list and articles turn page by page instead of scrolling. Extrablatt picks this by itself. Choose here if it picked wrong. The choice is kept in a cookie on this device.
