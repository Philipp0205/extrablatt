Allow Extrablatt to send books to this Kindle

Amazon only delivers articles if our sender address is on your approved list. Two steps, easiest on a phone:

  1. Find your Send-to-Kindle e-mail on Amazon under Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings, and enter it above.
  2. In the same place, add mail@extrablatt.app to the Approved Personal Document E-mail List.
