Amazon setup instructions

To let articles reach your Kindle, add our sender address to Amazon's approved list:

  1. Find your Send-to-Kindle e-mail on Amazon under Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings, and enter it above.
  2. In the same place, add sender@example.com to the Approved Personal Document E-mail List.

Open Amazon settings — easiest to complete on your phone.
