Your data

You can download everything we hold about this account, at any time and without asking. The file is JSON — readable in any text editor, and structured so another program can import it.

Download my data

What is in it

  • Your e-mail address, Kindle address, and when the account was created and confirmed
  • Your reading preference and, if you have one, your newsletter inbox address
  • Your subscription, if any: plan, status, renewal date, and your payment provider's identifiers for you
  • Every feed you follow
  • Every article we hold, with its title, address, and whether you read or sent it
  • Every Kindle delivery, with the time it happened
  • Any cancellation you have declared

What is not, and why

  • Article text. Titles and addresses are included, but not the stored text of each article: that is the publisher's writing rather than data about you, and it is still at the address in the file.
  • Your password. We only ever store a bcrypt hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the password.
  • Card details. They never reach us. Your payment provider holds those, and you can ask them for a copy separately.

How long we keep things

  • Your account, feeds and articles: until you delete the account
  • Kindle delivery history: 730 days
  • Cached article text, once read: 365 days, then re-fetched from the original address when you next open it
  • Used confirmation and password-reset links: 30 days
  • Payment records: as long as tax and commercial law require, which outlasts the account. The raw data our payment provider sends us is emptied after 90 days.

The privacy notice explains what we do with all of it. To correct anything, or to object to how it is used, write to us at the address on the imprint page.
