Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026.

Extrablatt is a self-serve reader that emails articles to your Kindle. This policy describes what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Account e-mail and a hashed password so you can log in.
  • Your Kindle e-mail address, used only to deliver documents you request.
  • The feeds you add and the articles they contain, so the app can show and send them.
  • Your subscription status — which plan you are on, when the current period ends, and the identifiers our payment provider uses for you. We do not receive or store card numbers.
  • Cancellations you declare, including the date and time and anything you wrote in the form. The law requires us to be able to confirm a cancellation and its exact time, so this is kept even after the subscription has ended.

What we do with it

  • We fetch the feed and article URLs you add in order to display and send them.
  • We send e-mail (account confirmation, password reset, and the articles you send to Kindle) through a third-party e-mail provider.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • If you subscribe, we pass your e-mail address to our payment provider so it can take the payment and issue your invoice. It processes that data as an independent controller under its own privacy notice, and it — not us — handles your card details.

How long we keep it

Account data lasts as long as the account. Payment and cancellation records are kept for as long as tax and commercial law require us to keep them, which is longer than the account itself; after that they are deleted.

Who we are, and where

Extrablatt is operated by Kubri by Philipp Kurrle und Ali Abriani KLG, a Swiss general partnership registered in Zürich. The full details are on the imprint page.

For anything about your data — a copy, a correction, a deletion, or an objection — write to hello@extrablatt.app. That reaches Philipp Kurrle in Stuttgart, Germany, who runs the service.

Because we are based in Switzerland, your data is processed outside the European Union. Switzerland has an adequacy decision from the European Commission, so no further safeguard is needed for the transfer itself.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to processing, or complain to a data protection authority in the country you live in.

Cookies

We use four cookies. All of them are needed to provide what you asked for, none of them tracks you, and there is no advertising or analytics cookie anywhere in this app — which is why you are not being asked to agree to any of it.

Getting a copy of your data

Under Settings → Your data you can download everything we hold about your account as a file, at any time and without asking us. It lists what is included and what is not.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings; this removes your account, feeds, and stored articles.

One exception is required by law: if you have ever paid or cancelled, the record of that payment or cancellation is kept for as long as tax and commercial law say it must be. It is unlinked from your account when you delete it.
