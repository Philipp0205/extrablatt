Send-to-Kindle

Amazon setup instructions

To let articles reach your Kindle, add our sender address to Amazon's approved list:

  1. Find your Send-to-Kindle e-mail on Amazon under Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings, and enter it above.
  2. In the same place, add sender@example.com to the Approved Personal Document E-mail List.

Open Amazon settings — easiest to complete on your phone.

Reading

Feeds are asked for as many entries as they will give. How those articles become read is up to you.

When this is on, paging past a list of articles marks them read — the same as tapping Next page at the end of a Kindle screen. When it is off, new feed articles arrive already marked read, so a refresh does not fill Unread with a backlog, and Next page only loads the next articles.
