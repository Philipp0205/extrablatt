Welcome! Two quick steps to start sending articles to your Kindle:

  1. Open Settings and enter your Send-to-Kindle e-mail (it ends in @kindle.com). Amazon lists it under Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings.
  2. Add our sender address mail@extrablatt.app to Amazon's Approved Personal Document E-mail List. Open your Amazon content & devices settings — this is easiest to do on your phone.
