Right of withdrawal

For consumers. Art. 246a EGBGB, §§ 355 ff. BGB.

Your right

You have 14 days to withdraw from a subscription contract, without giving any reason. The period begins on the day the contract is concluded.

To withdraw, tell us clearly that you do. An e-mail to hello@extrablatt.app is enough; so is the cancellation page, where you can say that you are withdrawing in the reason field. Sending the notice before the 14 days are up is all that is needed to be in time.

What happens then

We refund every payment we have received from you, without undue delay and at the latest within 14 days of being told, using the same means of payment you used.

When it ends early, and what you pay

Because you asked us to start straight away, and confirmed that you understood what that means, your right of withdrawal ends once the service has been provided in full — for a subscription, that is the end of the period you paid for.

If you withdraw after the service has started but before then, you pay a proportionate amount for the part you have used, and we refund the rest. For example, withdrawing one month into a yearly plan leaves you paying for that one month.

Cancelling instead

Withdrawal is not the same thing as cancelling. You can cancel at any time, with or without a reason, on the cancellation page; cancelling ends the subscription at the end of the period you have paid for, rather than unwinding it.
