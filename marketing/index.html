How to open Extrablatt on your Kindle

Extrablatt runs in the Kindle's own browser — Amazon calls it Web Browser, older firmware still says Experimental Browser. There is nothing to install: you type the address on the Kindle keyboard.

  1. Open the browser on your Kindle

    With Wi‑Fi on, tap the top of the Home screen so the toolbar appears, open the three-dot menu (⋮) and choose Web Browser. On older Kindles that entry is called Experimental Browser.

  2. Enter the address

    Type it into the browser's address bar:

    reader.extrablatt.app

  3. Create an account

    Sign up with an e-mail and password and confirm your address — on the Kindle, or from any other device. The first week is the full plan, at no charge.

  4. Follow sites and send articles

    Pick a few newspapers to start, or paste a site's normal address. Save your Kindle e-mail under Settings, then tap Send to Kindle on any article.

Built for a device that isn't a phone

Straight to your library

Every article is sent to your Kindle's Send-to-Kindle address — it shows up in your library like any other book.

Paged, not scrolled

Articles and lists are laid out a screenful at a time. Turning a page is one instant repaint instead of a slow, laggy scroll on e-ink.

Follow a site by pasting its address

Paste a normal website address and Extrablatt finds its news for you — you don't need a special link.

Clean, distraction-free text

Full article content is extracted and sanitized, with images off by default, so you get the writing without the surrounding clutter.

Your reading list, your account

Every account is fully isolated — the sites you follow, what you have read, and your history belong to you and nobody else.

No ads, ever

Nothing about you is for sale and no advertiser pays for this. Reading is free; sending to your Kindle after the first week is what a subscription pays for, because every article sent is an e-mail that costs real money to deliver.
